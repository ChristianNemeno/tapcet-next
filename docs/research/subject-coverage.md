## English / Grammar

Tested in: **All 7 exams**

### Topics
- Subject-verb agreement
- Pronoun reference and case
- Verb tense consistency
- Modifiers (dangling and misplaced)
- Parallel structure
- Punctuation (comma, semicolon, apostrophe)
- Capitalization rules
- Commonly confused words (affect/effect, its/it's, etc.)
- Sentence correction and completion

### Notes
- Grammar is the single most universal topic across all exams
- ACET has a dedicated Vocabulary section (synonyms, antonyms, analogies)
- PUPCET includes "Improving Paragraphs" — paragraph-level grammar editing

---

## Reading Comprehension

Tested in: **All 7 exams**

### Topics
- Identifying main idea and theme
- Locating supporting details
- Making inferences and drawing conclusions
- Understanding author's purpose and tone
- Evaluating arguments
- Vocabulary in context (understanding words from surrounding text)
- Literary devices: irony, metaphor, imagery

### Passage Types
- News and informational articles
- Science and social science passages
- Literary excerpts: short stories, essays, poetry
- Historical documents and speeches

### Notes
- UPCAT includes both English and **Filipino** reading passages
- Passage difficulty scales with the exam's target school prestige
- Speed is critical — passages are long, time is short

## Mathematics

Tested in: **UPCAT, ACET, USTET, DLSUCET, DOST, JLSS**

### Arithmetic
- Operations with integers, fractions, decimals
- Ratios, rates, and proportions
- Percentages: markup, discount, tax, tip
- Number properties: factors, multiples, prime numbers

### Algebra
- Simplifying algebraic expressions
- Solving linear equations and inequalities
- Systems of linear equations (substitution, elimination)
- Quadratic equations (factoring, quadratic formula)
- Exponents and radicals
- Polynomials: addition, subtraction, multiplication, factoring

### Geometry
- Lines, angles, and transversals
- Triangle properties: Pythagorean theorem, similarity, congruence
- Circle: circumference, area, arc, sector, chord, tangent
- Polygons: perimeter and area
- Solid figures: surface area and volume (cube, cylinder, sphere, cone)
- Coordinate geometry: slope, distance, midpoint, line equation

### Trigonometry (UPCAT primarily)
- Basic trigonometric ratios (SOH-CAH-TOA)
- Right triangle applications
- Unit circle basics

### Statistics and Probability
- Mean, median, mode, range
- Weighted average
- Simple probability
- Basic combinatorics (permutations, combinations)
- Interpreting graphs, charts, and tables

### Word Problems
- Age, rate, distance-time-speed, work problems
- Mixture and solution problems
- Geometry application problems
- Profit/loss, interest, percent change

## Science

Tested in: **UPCAT, USTET, DLSUCET, DOST, JLSS** (not ACET or PUPCET)

### Biology
- Cell structure and function (organelles, cell membrane, transport)
- Cell division: mitosis and meiosis
- DNA structure, replication, transcription, translation
- Mendelian genetics and inheritance patterns
- Evolution: natural selection, adaptation, speciation
- Ecological relationships: food chains, webs, nutrient cycles
- Human organ systems: digestive, respiratory, circulatory, nervous, endocrine, reproductive
- Classification of living things (taxonomy)
- Photosynthesis and cellular respiration

### Chemistry
- Atomic structure: protons, neutrons, electrons, isotopes
- Periodic table: groups, periods, trends
- Chemical bonding: ionic, covalent, metallic, hydrogen bonds
- Chemical reactions: types (synthesis, decomposition, single/double replacement, combustion)
- Balancing chemical equations
- Acids, bases, and pH scale
- Stoichiometry: mole concept, molar mass, limiting reagent
- States of matter and gas laws
- Solutions: concentration, solubility

### Physics
- Kinematics: speed, velocity, acceleration, displacement
- Newton's Laws of Motion
- Forces: friction, gravity, tension, normal force
- Work, energy, and power
- Simple machines and mechanical advantage
- Waves: properties, types, reflection, refraction
- Sound and light
- Electric charge, current, voltage, resistance (Ohm's Law)
- Basic circuits (series and parallel)
- Magnetism

### Earth Science
- Structure of the Earth (crust, mantle, core)
- Plate tectonics: earthquakes, volcanoes, faults
- Rock cycle: igneous, sedimentary, metamorphic
- Atmosphere: layers, weather patterns, climate
- Hydrosphere: water cycle, ocean currents
- Basic astronomy: solar system, stars, phases of the moon

## Abstract / Logical Reasoning

Tested in: **All 7 exams**

### Number and Letter Series
- Identify the next term in arithmetic/geometric sequences
- Mixed number-letter patterns

### Figural Analogies
- Identify the relationship between two figures and apply it to find a third
- Shape rotation, reflection, size change

### Matrix Patterns
- Complete a 3×3 grid based on row/column logic
- Shape + attribute matrices (color, size, number of sides)

### Logical Reasoning
- Deductive reasoning: if-then statements, syllogisms
- Argument validity and soundness
- Cause-and-effect relationships

### Spatial Reasoning
- Mental rotation of 2D and 3D figures
- Folding and unfolding patterns
- Counting faces, edges, vertices of solids

## Filipino Language

Tested in: **UPCAT only**

### Topics
- Filipino grammar: pangnangalan, panghalip, pandiwa, pang-uri, pang-abay
- Sentence construction and correction in Filipino
- Reading comprehension of Filipino texts
- Philippine literature: selected works and authors
- Figurative language in Filipino: tayutay, simili, metapor

---

## General Information

Tested in: **PUPCET only**

### Topics
- Philippine history: key events, figures, dates
- Philippine geography: provinces, regions, bodies of water
- Government and political structure
- Current events and news literacy
- Science and technology awareness
- International organizations and affairs

---

## Mechanical-Technical

Tested in: **DOST Merit, JLSS only**

### Topics
- Simple machines: levers, pulleys, wheels, gears, inclined planes
- Mechanical advantage calculations
- Reading mechanical diagrams and schematics
- Gear ratios and torque concepts
- Fluid pressure basics
- Basic structural concepts

---

## Vocabulary (Standalone)

Tested as a distinct section in: **ACET only** (others integrate it into English/Reading)

### Topics
- Synonyms and antonyms
- Word analogies (Doctor : Hospital :: Pilot : **Cockpit**)
- Multiple meanings of words
- Etymology and root words
- Context clue usage
