# USTET — University of Santo Tomas Entrance Test

## Overview

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| Full Name | University of Santo Tomas Entrance Test |
| Administering Body | UST Office of the Registrar |
| For | Incoming freshmen to UST undergraduate programs |
| Application Fee | PHP 600 (Filipinos in PH) / PHP 1,000 (abroad or foreign nationals) |
| Rescheduling Fee | PHP 200 |

---

## Test Format

| Section | Items | Time |
|---------|-------|------|
| English | 45 | 45 min |
| Mathematics | 60 | 45 min |
| Science | 80 | 45 min |
| Mental Ability | 80 | 30 min |
| **Total** | **265** | **~2.5 hrs** |

- **Question format:** Multiple choice (4 options)
- **No essay component**
- **Scoring:** Standard (no right-minus-wrong penalty)

---

## Scoring System

**USTET Final Rating Formula:**

```
Final Rating = (USTET Score × 80%) + (High School Average × 20%)
```

- Heavily test-weighted (80%) compared to UPCAT (60%)
- Each program/college sets its own cut-off score
- Cut-offs set by Academic Units per college, not published publicly
- Results available online via the USTET portal

## Subject Coverage

### English (45 items)
- Grammar and correct usage
- Vocabulary: synonyms, antonyms, analogies
- Sentence completion and correction
- Reading comprehension passages
- Punctuation and capitalization

### Mathematics (60 items)
- Arithmetic: integers, fractions, decimals, ratios, percentages
- Algebra: equations, inequalities, exponents, polynomials
- Geometry: triangles, circles, polygons, coordinate geometry
- Statistics and probability
- Word problems

### Science (80 items)
- **Biology:** Cell structure, genetics, evolution, ecology, human physiology
- **Chemistry:** Atomic structure, periodic table, reactions, stoichiometry, acids/bases
- **Physics:** Motion, forces, energy, waves, electricity
- **Earth Science:** Atmosphere, hydrosphere, geology, space

### Mental Ability (80 items — 30 min)
- Number series and letter series
- Abstract pattern recognition
- Logical reasoning and deduction
- Analogies (verbal and figural)
- Spatial reasoning

---

## Strategy Notes

- Science has the most items (80) — deepest coverage required
- Mental Ability section is the shortest in time relative to items (80 items / 30 min = ~22.5 sec/item)
- Final rating is 80% test score — high school grades have limited impact
- Multiple test dates allow flexible scheduling; apply early for preferred slots
- No guessing penalty — always answer every question
