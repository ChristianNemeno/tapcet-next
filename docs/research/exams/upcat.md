# UPCAT — University of the Philippines College Admissions Test

## Overview

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| Full Name | University of the Philippines College Admissions Test |
| Administering Body | UP Office of Admissions |
| For | Incoming freshmen to any UP campus nationwide |
| Application Fee | **Free** for Filipino applicants (RA 10931) |
| Foreign Applicants | ~USD 50 non-refundable |
| Acceptance Rate | ~10% (≈15,000 slots from 100,000+ takers) |
| Difficulty | Highest among major Philippine CETs |

---

## Test Format

| Section | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Language Proficiency | English and Filipino |
| Reading Comprehension | English and Filipino passages |
| Science | Biology, Chemistry, Physics, Earth Science |
| Mathematics | Algebra, Geometry, Trigonometry, Statistics |

- **Total duration:** ~4 hours
- **Question format:** Multiple choice (4 options)
- **Scoring:** Right-minus-wrong — incorrect answers deduct points
- **Languages tested:** Both English and Filipino (unique among major CETs)

---

## Scoring System

**University Predicted Grade (UPG)**

```
UPG = (UPCAT Score × 60%) + (High School GPA × 40%)
```

- Lower UPG = better (graded like a Philippine GPA where 1.0 is highest)
- Each campus and course has its own UPG cutoff
- Highly competitive programs (Medicine, Law, Engineering) have very low cutoffs
- Results typically released ~10 months after the exam

## Subject Coverage

### Language Proficiency & Reading Comprehension
- Grammar (English and Filipino)
- Vocabulary and word usage
- Sentence structure and correction
- Reading passages: literary, informational, scientific
- Inference, main idea, tone, author's purpose

### Mathematics
- Number theory and operations
- Algebraic expressions and equations
- Linear and quadratic equations
- Geometry: angles, triangles, circles, area, volume
- Trigonometry basics
- Probability and statistics
- Word problems and data interpretation

### Science
- **Biology:** Cell biology, genetics, evolution, ecology, human body systems
- **Chemistry:** Atomic structure, periodic table, bonding, reactions, acids/bases
- **Physics:** Mechanics, waves, energy, electricity, magnetism
- **Earth Science:** Atmosphere, geology, astronomy

---

## Strategy Notes

- **Right-minus-wrong scoring** — blind guessing is risky; strategic elimination is preferred
- Time pressure is significant; practice pacing is essential
- Both English and Filipino proficiency must be prepared
- High school GPA is 40% of the final UPG — academic performance throughout HS matters
- Students applying to UP should also have high school verified by school officials (Form 2B)
