# PUPCET — Polytechnic University of the Philippines College Entrance Test

## Overview

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| Full Name | Polytechnic University of the Philippines College Entrance Test |
| Administering Body | PUP Admissions Office |
| For | High school graduates and equivalents applying to any PUP campus |
| Application Portal | pup.edu.ph/iapply/PUPCET |
| Annual Applicants | ~50,000 |
| Available Slots | ~8,000 |
| Acceptance Rate | ~16% (most competitive by volume) |
| Tuition | Free ("Iskolar ng Bayan" program) |

---

## Test Format

| Section | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Language Proficiency | Grammar, vocabulary, sentence completion |
| Reading Comprehension | Multi-genre passages |
| Abstract Reasoning | Patterns, logical relationships |
| General Information | General knowledge, current events |
| Improving Paragraphs | Paragraph editing, grammar correction |
| **Total** | **70+ items** |

- **Total duration:** ~2–3 hours (exact breakdown not published)
- **Question format:** Multiple choice (4 options)
- **Scoring:** Standard (no penalty for wrong answers)

---

## Scoring System

- Score determines admission eligibility across all PUP campuses
- Specific cut-off scores not publicly published
- Highly competitive despite no official passing score
- Campus-specific results released March–April

---

## Schedule (Typical)

| Event | Typical Period |
|-------|----------------|
| Online applications open | July–August |
| Exam date | November–December |
| Results release | March–April (campus-specific) |

- Application via PUP iApply website
- Applicants may apply and test at **only one PUP campus** per academic year
- Applying to multiple campuses results in voided results

---

## Eligibility Requirements

- High school graduates
- PEPT passers with average rating of 82 or higher
- ALS A&E Test passers with Standard Score of 100 or higher
- Filipino and foreign high school graduates
- No age restriction specified

---

## Subject Coverage

### Language Proficiency
- Grammar: subject-verb agreement, pronoun usage, verb tenses
- Vocabulary: synonyms, antonyms, word usage in context
- Sentence completion and correction

### Reading Comprehension
- Multi-genre passages: news articles, essays, short fiction, informational texts
- Main idea and supporting details
- Inference, tone, author's purpose
- Vocabulary in context

### Abstract Reasoning
- Number and letter series
- Figural analogies and matrix patterns
- Logical sequences and deduction

### General Information
- Philippine history and geography
- Current events and news literacy
- Basic civic knowledge

### Improving Paragraphs
- Paragraph coherence and organization
- Identifying errors in grammar, punctuation, spelling
- Rewriting sentences for clarity and style

---

## Strategy Notes

- **PUPCET is unique** in including General Information and Improving Paragraphs — sections rarely emphasized in other exams
- No science or mathematics sections — unlike UPCAT, ACET, USTET, DLSUCET
- Students from public high schools make up the majority of applicants
- Free tuition is a major draw; competition is extreme for the cost-benefit
- **One-campus rule** is strictly enforced — choose carefully
