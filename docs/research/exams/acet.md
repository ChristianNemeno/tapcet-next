# ACET — Ateneo College Entrance Test

## Overview

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| Full Name | Ateneo College Entrance Test |
| Administering Body | Ateneo de Manila University, Office of Admissions |
| For | Incoming freshmen and transfer applicants to AdMU |
| Application Fee | PHP 600 (Filipinos in PH) / PHP 2,500 (abroad) |
| Fee Exemption | Students from public or science high schools |
| Difficulty | Very high; known for extreme time pressure |

---

## Test Format

| Section | Format |
|---------|--------|
| English Proficiency | Grammar, vocabulary, sentence structure |
| Mathematics Proficiency | Algebra, geometry, basic statistics |
| Abstract Reasoning | Patterns, analogies, logical sequences |
| Reading Comprehension | Literary and non-literary passages |
| Vocabulary Proficiency | Word meanings, relationships, usage |
| Essay | Written response to a prompt |

- **Total duration:** ~4+ hours
- **Question format:** Multiple choice (4 options) + essay
- **No calculator** permitted; scratch paper provided
- **Scoring:** Weighted alongside high school grades for admissions

---

## Scoring System

- No single published formula (unlike USTET or UPCAT)
- Essay is scored separately by Ateneo evaluators
- Final admission decision combines ACET score + HS grades + essay
- No explicit passing score; it's competitive ranking
- Results released in May following the test

## Subject Coverage

### English Proficiency
- Subject-verb agreement, pronoun reference, modifiers
- Parallel structure, sentence clarity
- Punctuation and capitalization
- Commonly misused words

### Mathematics Proficiency
- Arithmetic: fractions, decimals, ratios, percentages
- Algebra: linear and quadratic equations, inequalities, polynomials
- Geometry: triangles, circles, coordinate geometry
- Statistics: mean, median, mode, basic probability
- No trigonometry emphasis (unlike UPCAT)

### Abstract Reasoning
- Number sequences and letter series
- Figural analogy and pattern matrices
- Logical relationships and deduction

### Reading Comprehension
- Identifying main idea, supporting details, inferences
- Tone, mood, author's purpose
- Literary devices and techniques
- Both literary and expository passages

### Vocabulary Proficiency
- Synonyms and antonyms
- Word analogies (e.g., "Doctor : Hospital :: Teacher : ___")
- Context clues and usage

### Essay
- Timed written response to a given prompt
- Evaluated on content, organization, grammar, and mechanics
- Unique to ACET among major CETs

---

## Strategy Notes

- **Time pressure is the primary challenge** — most test-takers cannot finish all sections
- Practice skipping and returning to difficult questions
- Strong vocabulary is disproportionately important across multiple sections
- Essay must be prepared separately — structured writing under time constraints
- Public/science HS students are fee-exempt; this signals Ateneo's reach beyond elite schools
