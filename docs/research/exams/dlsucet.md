# DLSUCET / DCAT — De La Salle University College Entrance Test

## Overview

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| Full Name | De La Salle University College Entrance Test (DLSUCET) / De La Salle College Admission Test (DCAT) |
| Administering Body | DLSU Office of Admissions and Scholarships |
| For | Incoming freshmen and transfer applicants to DLSU |
| Contact | (02) 8523-4230 / admission.requirements@dlsu.edu.ph |

---

## Test Format

| Section | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Mathematics | Arithmetic, algebra, geometry, statistics |
| Science | Biology, chemistry, physics |
| English | Reading comprehension, vocabulary, grammar |
| General Information / Mental Ability | Abstract reasoning, general knowledge |
| **Total** | **280 items** |

- **Total duration:** ~2–3 hours
- **Question format:** Multiple choice only — no essay
- **Scoring:** No right-minus-wrong penalty (unlike UPCAT)

---

## Scoring System

- Straightforward: all correct answers count, no deduction for wrong answers
- **Key difference from UPCAT:** DLSUCET encourages answering every question
- Specific score formula not publicly disclosed
- Combined with high school performance for final admissions decision
- Different programs may have different cut-off thresholds

## Subject Coverage

### Mathematics
- Number theory: integers, fractions, decimals, ratios, proportions
- Algebra: linear equations, quadratic equations, systems, exponents
- Geometry: angles, triangles, circles, area, volume, coordinate geometry
- Statistics: mean, median, mode, probability, data interpretation
- Word problems and multi-step problems

### Science
- **Biology:** Cell theory, genetics, taxonomy, ecology, human systems
- **Chemistry:** Matter and energy, atomic theory, periodic table, chemical bonding, reactions
- **Physics:** Kinematics, dynamics, energy, waves, electricity and magnetism

### English
- Reading comprehension: inference, main idea, author's purpose
- Vocabulary: synonyms, antonyms, context clues, analogies
- Grammar: subject-verb agreement, pronoun usage, modifiers, parallelism
- Sentence correction and completion

### General Information / Mental Ability
- Abstract reasoning: figural and numerical patterns
- Logical sequences and analogies
- Spatial visualization

---

## Strategy Notes

- **No guessing penalty** — always answer every question, even if unsure
- 280 items is the highest item count of major CETs — time management is critical
- No essay means all preparation is multiple-choice focused
- DLSUCET and DCAT may refer to the same test in different contexts (regular vs. special admissions)
