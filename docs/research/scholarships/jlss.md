# JLSS — DOST-SEI Junior Level Science Scholarship

## Overview

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| Full Name | DOST-SEI Junior Level Science Scholarships (JLSS) Program |
| Administering Body | Department of Science and Technology – Science Education Institute |
| For | Regular 2nd-year college students in STEM programs at DOST-recognized universities |
| Application Fee | **Free** |
| Exam Fee | **Free** |

---

## Key Distinction from DOST Merit

- **DOST Merit Scholarship** — targets graduating HS seniors (before college)
- **JLSS** — targets current 2nd-year college students (mid-degree scholarship)
- Both are administered by DOST-SEI but cover different student populations

---

## Eligibility Requirements

- Regular 2nd-year college student (equivalent to 3rd-year standing)
- Enrolled in a DOST-SEI priority S&T course
- General Weighted Average (GWA) of at least **83%** from 1st year through 1st semester of 2nd year
- No conditional or failing grades during that period
- Enrolled at a DOST-recognized college or university

---

## Test Format

| Part | Sections |
|------|----------|
| **Intellective** | Logical Reasoning, English, Science, Mathematics, Mechanical-Technical |
| **Non-Intellective** | Self-Inventory (personality/values — not scored) |

- **Question format:** Multiple choice (rigorous aptitude test)
- Three timed parts (exact duration not published)
- Application via: e-Scholarship Application System (online)

---

## Scoring System

- Five intellective domains scored and weighted
- Self-Inventory is assessed for program fit, not scored
- GWA from prior college years also considered
- Combined evaluation determines scholarship award

---

## Schedule (Typical)

| Event | Typical Period |
|-------|----------------|
| Application opens | March–April |
| Applications close | June |
| Exam | July (e.g., July 27, 2025) |
| Results | After evaluation period |

- Regional DOST offices coordinate applications
- Applications submitted via e-Scholarship online system

---

## Subject Coverage

Same six domains as DOST Merit Scholarship:

### Logical Reasoning
- Deductive and inductive reasoning
- Syllogisms and argument analysis

### English
- Grammar, vocabulary, reading comprehension

### Science
- Higher-level biology, chemistry, physics (college-level topics expected)
- Applied science problems

### Mathematics
- College-level algebra, calculus basics, statistics
- Engineering mathematics may be relevant for STEM majors

### Mechanical-Technical
- Mechanical aptitude and technical diagrams
- Simple machines, spatial reasoning, engineering concepts

### Self-Inventory (Non-Intellective)
- Values and attitudes toward science
- Personality assessment for scholarship fit

---

## Scholarship Benefits

| Benefit | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| Tuition | Full coverage for remaining years |
| Monthly stipend | Provided |
| Book allowance | Annual |
| Thesis allowance | One-time for graduating year |
| Connectivity | Learning support |

- Renewal requires maintaining minimum academic standards
- Scholars must remain enrolled in DOST priority courses

---

## Strategy Notes

- GWA cutoff (83%) makes this competitive even before the exam
- College-level science and math preparation required (more advanced than DOST Merit)
- Mechanical-Technical section still present — often overlooked
- Since this targets current college students, preparation can be more targeted by major
