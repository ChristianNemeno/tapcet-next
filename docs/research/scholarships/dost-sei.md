# DOST-SEI Merit Scholarship (Undergraduate)

## Overview

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| Full Name | DOST-SEI Undergraduate Merit Scholarship Program |
| Administering Body | Department of Science and Technology – Science Education Institute |
| Established | 1958 (formerly NSDB/NSTA Scholarship) |
| For | Graduating HS seniors pursuing STEM courses at DOST-recognized universities |
| Application Fee | **Free** |
| Exam Fee | **Free** |

---

## Eligibility Requirements

- Natural-born Filipino citizen
- Graduating senior high school student
- Strong academic record, especially in Science and Mathematics
- Family gross income within regional guidelines (verified)
- Not yet enrolled in any post-secondary institution

---

## Test Format

| Part | Sections |
|------|----------|
| **Intellective** | Logical Reasoning, English, Science, Mathematics, Mechanical-Technical |
| **Non-Intellective** | Self-Inventory (personality/values — no right/wrong answers) |

- **Total duration:** ~3 hours (including instructions)
- **Question format:** Multiple choice, pencil-and-paper
- Application via: e-Scholarship Application System (online)

---

## Scoring System

- Intellective section measures academic aptitude and mental alertness
- Non-Intellective (Self-Inventory) is used to assess program fit — not scored pass/fail
- Combined score determines scholarship award ranking
- High performance in Science and Mathematics is weighted heavily

---

## Schedule (Typical)

| Event | Typical Period |
|-------|----------------|
| Application opens | Late April – May |
| Registration deadline | Mid-year (varies by DOST region) |
| Exam | July–August |
| Results | End of year / early next year |

- Regional DOST offices manage applications and testing
- Applications submitted via the e-Scholarship system

---

## Subject Coverage

### Logical Reasoning
- Deductive and inductive reasoning
- Syllogisms and logical arguments
- Critical thinking problems

### English
- Grammar and correct usage
- Vocabulary and word analogies
- Reading comprehension

### Science
- Biology, Chemistry, Physics, Earth Science
- Conceptual and applied science problems
- Heavy emphasis on Grade 9–10 curriculum level

### Mathematics
- Arithmetic, algebra, geometry
- Word problems and multi-step solutions
- Basic statistics and probability

### Mechanical-Technical
- Mechanical aptitude: levers, pulleys, gears, simple machines
- Technical diagrams and spatial reasoning
- Basic engineering concepts

### Self-Inventory (Non-Intellective)
- Personality traits, values, attitudes
- Study habits and interests
- No correct answers — used for scholarship fit assessment

---

## Scholarship Benefits

| Benefit | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| Tuition | Full coverage |
| Monthly stipend | Provided throughout the program |
| Book allowance | Annual |
| Thesis allowance | One-time for graduating year |
| Transportation | Included |
| Connectivity | Learning and connectivity support |

- Duration: 4–5 years depending on program
- Renewal: Based on academic performance (minimum GWA required)
- Scholars must pursue DOST-listed priority S&T courses

---

## Priority STEM Courses Covered

Agriculture, Fisheries, Forestry, Engineering (all branches), Computer Science, Information Technology, Mathematics, Statistics, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Environmental Science, Food Technology, Nursing, Medical Technology, Pharmacy, and other allied sciences.

---

## Strategy Notes

- Mechanical-Technical section is unique to DOST exams — rarely prepared for
- Self-Inventory answers should reflect genuine interests in science and service
- Income verification is required — scholarship is need-and-merit-based
- Regional offices have slightly different deadlines; check local DOST office
